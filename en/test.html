CEFR Levels

We align with the international Common European Framework of Reference

A1 - Beginner

Can introduce yourself, ask simple questions, and understand basic phrases.

A2 - Elementary

Can communicate in routine tasks, describe your background, and understand simple texts.

B1 - Intermediate

Can deal with most travel situations, write simple texts, and describe experiences and ambitions.

B2 - Upper Intermediate

Can interact with native speakers fluently, write detailed texts, and understand complex topics.

C1 - Advanced

Can express ideas fluently, use language flexibly for social and professional purposes.

Our Programs

🎓

Scholarship Fund

Full and partial scholarships for talented students from low-income families. We believe financial barriers should never limit potential.

  • Academic excellence scholarships
  • Leadership & talent scholarships
  • Community service scholarships
📚

Free English Classes

Weekly free classes for underprivileged children in Tinh Bien and surrounding areas. No fees, no requirements — just a desire to learn.

  • Community center partnerships
  • Volunteer teacher program
  • Free learning materials provided
🤝

Sponsorship Program

Partner with local organizations to sponsor English education for entire communities, schools, or orphanages.

  • Corporate CSR partnerships
  • Individual donor program
  • Annual charity events
🌱

Teacher Training

Free training workshops for public school English teachers in An Giang province. Sharing our methodology for wider impact.

  • Annual teacher conferences
  • Online resource sharing
  • Peer mentoring networks

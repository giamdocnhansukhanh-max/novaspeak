About NovaCenter

From a dream in Western Vietnam to a nationwide English education ecosystem

The Meaning Behind NovaCenter

⭐

Nova

From Latin "novus" meaning new. A new approach to English learning, a new methodology, a new ecosystem for Western Vietnam.

📍

Center

A hub — a meeting point, a connection center, a place where learning converges and confidence is born.

🚀

NovaCenter

The new center of English education in Western Vietnam — where innovation meets community, and every student finds their voice.

Vision & Mission

🎯

Our Mission

To build a generation of Vietnamese who communicate confidently in English, opening doors to global opportunities and breaking down language barriers.

🔭

Our Vision

To become the leading English education ecosystem in Western Vietnam, expanding nationwide through our franchise model by 2030.

Core Values: S.P.E.A.K

Five letters. Five principles. One commitment to excellence.

S

Speak First

Communication before perfection. Students speak 70% of class time from day one.

P

Passion

Passionate teachers create passionate learners. We hire for heart, train for skill.

E

Ecosystem

No learner is an island. We build networks of students, teachers, and partners.

A

Accountability

We measure progress, report transparently, and guarantee results.

K

Kindness

Mistakes are celebrated. Every voice matters. Learning is joyful.

Meet the Founder

K

Nguyen Van Tan Khanh

Founder & CEO

Born and raised in An Giang, Khanh witnessed firsthand how language barriers limited opportunities for young people in Western Vietnam. After years of teaching and observing outdated methods, he envisioned NovaCenter — a place where students don't just learn English, but live it. His philosophy is simple: "If you can speak, you can succeed."
